Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to find, read and use the information on this site.

Our standard

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, beyond the WCAG 2.0 Level AA requirement under Ontario's Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA).

  • Works with screen readers and keyboards, with a visible focus outline
  • Text and colours meet AA contrast in light and dark mode
  • Text enlarges to 200% and pages reflow on small screens
  • No moving content, time limits or autoplaying media
  • Illustrations have text descriptions

Ask for another format

We can provide information from this site in another format, including large print, plain text or French. If something on the site does not work for you, please tell us the page and what happened.
