Web resource

MyInCAT

Myogenic single-cell Integration of Cachexia Transcriptomics data

MyInCAT brings together single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-sequencing data from the skeletal muscle and tumours of mice with cancer cachexia, along with muscle and tumours from people with pancreatic cancer. Explore the integrated data in your browser to generate hypotheses, check your own findings and make publication-ready plots.

Open MyInCAT Download the data

What you can do

  • Visualize gene expression across cell types, subclusters and conditions
  • Run differential gene expression and pathway analyses
  • Explore predicted cell communication with CellChat, from tumour to muscle and within muscle
  • Export publication-ready plots

Associated publication

In press

Tumour signals propagate muscle inflammatory signalling and indirectly target myofibres in cancer cachexia

Brown A, Lafond I, De Lisio M, Wiper-Bergeron N. J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle. 2026;e70441. doi:10.1002/jcsm.70441

What we found

Across several mouse cancer models and in people with pancreatic cancer, tumours signal mainly to immune and other non-muscle cells in muscle, through TNF, CCL2/CCL12, CXCL12 and OSM. Those cells then amplify inflammatory signalling within the muscle.

Muscle fibres themselves receive little direct signal from tumours. Changes in their gene expression are better explained by the loss of signals for muscle maintenance, extracellular matrix and nerve-related support.

How to cite

If you use the integrated dataset, MyInCAT or its code, please cite:

Brown A, Lafond I, De Lisio M, Wiper-Bergeron N. Tumour signals propagate muscle inflammatory signalling and indirectly target myofibres in cancer cachexia. J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle. 2026;e70441. doi:10.1002/jcsm.70441

Please also cite the individual datasets and software packages used in your analyses. They are listed under References on the MyInCAT home page.

Related dataset

Muscle across the course of cancer cachexia

Single-cell RNA-sequencing of skeletal muscle from male mice at 2, 2.5 and 3.5 weeks after Lewis lung carcinoma (LLC) tumour implantation, with matched controls. It is one of the datasets integrated in MyInCAT.
